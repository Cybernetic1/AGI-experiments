Our new strategy is to use the game of TicTacToe as a simulation of AGI. We try to predict the game play of TTT as it is played by random computer moves. The "latent representation" of the autoencoder is in the form of logic propositions, and the logic rules are neural networks acting on them (there can be multiple layers). The entire set of logic propositions in all layers constitute the "state" of the Autoencoder and which will also be the "state" for reinforcement learning experiments later. The form of logic propositions are fixed by us, but AE can learn (invent) new predicates and constants. I started a new project in a new folder with Copilot CLI.
